Read the New Testament in 2026

Five minutes a day, five chapters a week. The plan began on 5 January 2026.

Follow on the Bible App →Download the plan →

One church, one book

We're reading the whole New Testament together in 2026.

Read one chapter a day, five days a week, and in one year you'll finish all 260 chapters of the New Testament.

Join us as we set aside the year to read the words of Jesus and his early followers. The New Testament is the most influential collection of writing in history, and we believe it is as relevant today as ever.

Sunday messages

Every Sunday in 2026 we preach from a passage in the five chapters we've just read together that week.

Watch messages →

Daily devotions

Every weekday at 6am we post a short devotion on that day's chapter. Follow along, comment and share it with others.

Read along each day

Find a regular time and a place away from distractions, and read along with us as we go deeper into God's word as a community.

Download the plan →

Discuss it and live it out

The best part of reading the same chapter each day is exploring it together. Share what stood out to you each week, and find a group where you can encourage one another to live it out.
