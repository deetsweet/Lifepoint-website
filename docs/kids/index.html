Welcome

A place where every kid belongs.

At Lifepoint Kids we want everyone to feel like they have a place where they belong, feel safe and can explore what it means to follow Jesus.

We play games, tell stories and chat about how to live with joy and hope with God.

First time at Lifepoint Kids?

Here's how check-in works.

When you arrive, look for the Lifepoint Kids sign in the courtyard. Our team will help register your kids. Each child receives a name tag sticker and you receive a parent sticker to keep. You'll need this parent sticker to collect your child at the end of the service.

Registration opens 10 minutes before our morning services. Please let us know about any allergies or additional needs at check-in.
