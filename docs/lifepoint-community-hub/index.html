The project

Where, what and why.

The location

In 2019 we purchased a 1.1 acre block on one of the biggest intersections in Moreton Bay.

The block sits at the crossroads of the three major regions of our mission: Deception Bay and Narangba to the north, Mango Hill, North Lakes and Kallangur to the south-west, and the Redcliffe Peninsula to the east.

Find the location →

The design

We want to create a place that feels like home, not a business. Spaces used all week long by the community, where people love to gather with their crew and experience God's beauty and love.

Stage 1 is for Monday to Saturday community and ministry events. Stage 2 will add a 300-seat auditorium for Sunday gatherings.

Features

  • 300-seat auditorium (Stage 2)
  • Spacious multipurpose room for kids and other ministries
  • Office, prayer room and meeting spaces
  • Kitchen and coffee area
  • Large playground for kids
  • Some sealed parking, plus reinforced turf for Sunday parking
  • A green courtyard with mature trees

Our vision

A city on a hill.

"You are the light of the world. A town built on a hill cannot be hidden." Matthew 5:14

For many years our church has met in rented facilities, and God has given us much growth and fruit for his kingdom in this season as a church family.

The time has now come for the next chapter of our gospel mission to this region. One part of this calling is pioneering our own permanent home for ministry, outreach and community engagement for generations to come.

We love our Moreton Bay region, and want every person to hear and experience the transforming hope and life found in making Jesus our King.
