Ask a question

Questions about giving

Let’s keep
it simple.

Do I need to give money to the church?

No. Giving is voluntary, never a condition of belonging. Christians give in response to God’s love and generosity, rather than out of pressure or obligation.

How much should I give?

Pray, consider your circumstances and choose an amount you can give willingly and responsibly. A tithe (10%) is a guide some Christians use, rather than a compulsory payment or a limit on generosity. See Malachi 3:10. You can also listen to our message on financial freedom.

Can I give regularly?

Yes. You can set up a recurring bank transfer using the account details above. For online giving, visit our ChurchSuite donation page to see the available options.

Can I give to the building fund or Lifepoint Caboolture West?

Yes. Both have their own bank account listed above. For the Building Fund, you can also complete a pledge form. Find out more about Lifepoint Caboolture West.

What does the Bible say about generosity?

Generosity flows from gratitude for what God has given us. 2 Corinthians 9:6-11 encourages willing, cheerful giving and trust in God’s provision. Giving is not a transaction or a promise of personal wealth; it is a way to worship God, care for others and share what we have.

How can I ask about church finances?

We welcome questions about giving, our budget and how funds are used. Contact our team and we’ll help you connect with the right person.
