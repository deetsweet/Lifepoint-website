Mission statement

Lifepoint exists to see thousands of lives transformed by Jesus.

Picture of Lifepoint at the end of 2030

In 2030, Lifepoint will be a healthy and vibrant family of four churches across the City of Moreton Bay that is known as a place where Jesus is transforming thousands of lives.

1200

We see 1200 people gathering weekly across four locations.

100 / 80

We are seeing 100 people decide to follow Jesus and 80 people baptised every year.

1000

We have a measurable discipleship pathway with 1000 people in our pathways taking clear next steps in faith.

Six markers

We are focused on six markers which form the core of our strategic commitments.

1Outward facing 2Praying 3Pathways 4Healthy 5Next-Gen focused 6Multiplying

1Marker 1 of 6

We are an outward facing church

Our mission is simple: thousands of lives transformed by Jesus. We design everything with people far from God in mind. Our language is clear, barriers are low, and next steps are simple. We resist the gravitational pull of focusing on insiders. Our highest goal is to offer the good news of Jesus to those who need it most: the lost.

We are known for being for our community by focusing on inner transformation. We offer accessible counselling, courses and mentoring to provide social, emotional and spiritual support.

We ensure all our gatherings, environments and digital spaces are welcoming, effective and prayerfully prepared to help people powerfully experience the love of God.

By 2030

  • Every year there are 100 people making a first time decision to follow Jesus who we have confirmed and have details for.
  • Every year there are 80 people baptised across our church campuses, with baptisms every First Sunday.
  • We have 200 participants for Alpha each year.
  • We have 100 people regularly serving in outreach events and groups that are a potential funnel into the Discovery pathway (e.g. COACH mentors, community outreach events, Alpha leaders, playgroups, EFC).

2Marker 2 of 6

We are a praying church

Lifepoint Church sees prayer as the most important work. Our primary battle is spiritual, and we wage war through the prayers of God's people. We have dedicated prayer spaces at each church campus. We are known as a praying church.

We teach people to pray. God's transforming presence is expected and experienced in our meetings. There is something powerful happening at every gathering that everyone can sense.

By 2030

  • We run ten weekly prayer meetings.
  • We run an annual prayer week in each location with over 50% of our Sunday attendance taking part.
  • We run a prayer course in our Growth Pathway with 100 unique completions per year.
  • We never run a church meeting or event without 15 minutes of prayer.

3Marker 3 of 6

We are a pathways church

Lifepoint is a pathways church, which means everything we do is designed to help people take their next step in faith and relationship with Jesus. We are known for moving people from pre-Christian to a Spirit-empowered, faith-filled disciple multiplier!

There are no standalone programs. There are no random ministries. There are no overlooked people in our church who don't have a next step in following Jesus.

Every pathway team is directed by a leader who owns clear Growth and Mission Objectives (God Desired Outcomes, or GDOs) within our pathway. Our people can name where they are in our pathway and what comes next for them.

The Lifepoint Pathway

The Lifepoint Pathway is our shared map for discipleship and mission.

  1. Front Doorhello!
  2. Tablejoin us!
  3. Living Roomgo deeper!
  4. Kitchenhelp us cook!

Every meeting, ministry, environment and event is created to move people through this pathway. Within our "house" pathway we have four clear pathways for both Next-Gen and Adults.

Connection
  1. Contact
  2. Connected (Group or Serving)
  3. Member
Discovery
  1. Alpha
  2. First Time Decision
  3. Foundations
  4. Baptism
Growth
  1. Baptised
  2. Serving / Small Group
  3. Freedom and Fruitfulness Programs
Leadership
  1. Step In (Leadership Training)
  2. Step Up (Ministry Responsibility and Mentoring)
  3. Step Out (Pastoral Residency)

By 2030

  • Connection Pathway: 3000 contacts, 720 connected (small group or serving), 200 members.
  • Discovery: 100 confirmed new people following Jesus and 80 baptisms every year.
  • Growth: metrics to be confirmed.
  • Leadership: Level 1 (Step In) 50, Level 2 (Step Up) 25, Level 3 (Step Out) 10 (to be confirmed).

4Marker 4 of 6

We are a healthy church

Lifepoint is full of broken people who are committed to becoming whole through the transforming power of the good news of King Jesus. Our leaders have healthy relationships with God, others and themselves, and they intentionally help our community to heal and grow into spiritual and emotional maturity in Jesus. We lead people to experience and practise healing in God's presence both corporately and privately, and we have multiple ministries and courses designed to help people live holistically in the abundant life of God.

We go at God's pace, promote rhythms of prayer, Scripture and sabbath, address issues with openness and authenticity, and prioritise our inner lives with God.

We pursue Jesus first, not church 'success'.

By 2030

  • 80% of our key leaders are self-identifying as healthy in our Key Leader Quarterly Health Survey.

5Marker 5 of 6

We are a next-gen focused church

Lifepoint Church is known for prioritising helping the next generation to become faith-filled followers of Jesus. We know the most important thing in life is not what we achieve, but who we raise. Our ministries and resourcing reflect our priority to be a safe yet challenging place for young people to both learn and practise following Jesus. We partner with parents to pass on faith to our children, give young leaders a go early, and make sure every young person who comes to Lifepoint is connected with a leader who knows and loves them.

In 2030 we recognise and are experiencing a move of God among our youth and young adults. We release young people to lead worship, preach, pray and carry the gospel with confidence and power. We are seeing hundreds of young people come into God's Kingdom and become missionaries to their peers.

By 2030

  • Every year we have 20 teenagers in Level 1 (Step In) of our Leadership pathway.
  • Next Gen Pathway: 200 children on Sunday mornings, 200 teenagers at Friday program, 200 young adults.
  • 75% of regular young people transition to the next age ministry (kids, youth, young adults).
  • 50 baptisms per year are Next-Gen (25 and under).

6Marker 6 of 6

We are a multiplying church

We multiply new believers, new leaders, and new churches.

New believers

We see new people deciding to follow Jesus every month. We teach and equip those already following Jesus how to invest and invite others to discover Jesus. We run Alpha every term. On the First Sunday of each month we present a clear gospel message and give testimonies of God transforming real lives.

By 2030

100 people every year give their lives to Jesus.

New leaders

Lifepoint is a leadership factory that encourages and equips new leaders for the church and the marketplace. We have a dedicated Leadership pathway with three levels of leadership. We are producing 4 new pastoral leaders every year who could plant, revitalise, or be sent to another church in need of leadership. We give people a go early, have high expectations and allow people to try things in a supportive and grace-filled environment.

By 2030

We are training 4 new pastoral leaders every year who are ready for staff deployment in a church or ministry size of 100 people with 10 volunteer leaders.

New churches

By 2030 we are a network of four church locations that are locally led with a Lifepoint Central team for vision, leadership development, systems and resourcing, alongside campus-based staff who shepherd local congregations. We are a family of churches who work together under agreed guidelines. We see multi-site church as an effective and unifying way to plant, revitalise and lead healthy churches that see the good news of Jesus reach their communities.

Each Lifepoint Campus is run by a Campus Pastor and team who have autonomy to lead within our agreed Lifepoint model, system and pathways. We have a clear and healthy balance of local autonomy and central leadership and alignment.

By 2030

We have 4 church locations.

Our mission explained

This mission statement is based on Jesus' instruction for his church to make disciples whose lives are changed by obedience to his teaching, to ultimately love God and love others as ourselves.

"Then Jesus came to them and said, 'All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptising them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.'"

Matthew 28:18-20 NIV

"Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the Law?" Jesus replied: "'Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.' This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: 'Love your neighbour as yourself.' All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments."

Matthew 22:36-40 NIV

Our values

We are...

An Inviting Family

A place you feel accepted and can't wait to invite others into.

Open
Anyone can belong and feel safe and thought of.
Vibrant
There is a tangible sense of relaxed, joyful energy and real life in our family.
Authentic
We are down to earth, honest and real. We don't change depending on who we are around.

A House of Prayer

A place where God is central, praised and needed.

Dependent
We prioritise our relationship with God. We know we can only give out what we have received.
Thankful
We are ridiculously thankful people who know all we have is because of God's grace, so we live in overflowing gratitude and worship.
Prayerful
We pray first, and always. We believe God loves our prayer as it changes us and genuinely changes our world. We live a lifestyle of prayer.

A Light to the World

A people who live to love others and point them to Jesus.

Generous
We give freely and abundantly of our time and resources without restraint or judgement, as it's all God's anyway.
Investors
We invest and serve wherever God has called us in our community, and we intentionally invest in the few to reach the many.
Inviters
We all love to invite people to discover Jesus and experience his love and his community.
