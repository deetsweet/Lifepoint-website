Our vision for 2030

Lifepoint in 2030

Thousands of lives transformed by Jesus. Here's what we believe God is building, and the part every one of us gets to play.

Why we exist

It's always been about people.

Jesus told his followers to go and make disciples, and promised to be with them to the very end. That's still our job. We're not here to build a bigger church for its own sake. We're here because people across Moreton Bay are far from God, and Jesus changes lives.

So our mission is simple: thousands of lives transformed by Jesus.

Read Matthew 28:18-20 →

The picture

By the end of 2030.

We see a healthy and vibrant family of churches across the City of Moreton Bay, known for community, transformation and authenticity.

1,200people gathering every week across our campuses
4locations across the City of Moreton Bay
100people choosing to follow Jesus every year
1new pastor raised up and sent out every year

Six markers

What we'll be known for.

These six markers shape every decision we make between now and 2030.

  1. 01

    We are outward facing

    We build everything with people far from God in mind. Clear language, low barriers and simple next steps. We refuse to drift towards looking after ourselves, because the best news we have is for the people who haven't heard it yet.

    In 2030Alpha every term, 200 guests a year, and baptisms every First Sunday.

  2. 02

    We are praying

    Prayer is the most important work we do. We expect God to show up when we gather, and we teach each other how to pray. Every campus has a place set apart for it.

    In 2030Ten prayer meetings every week and two prayer weeks a year in every location.

  3. 03

    We are a pathways church

    Nothing we run stands on its own. Every gathering, group and event is there to help someone take their next step with Jesus, and everyone in our church can name theirs.

    In 2030Four clear pathways (connection, discovery, growth and leadership), and nobody overlooked.

  4. 04

    We are healthy

    We're broken people being made whole by Jesus. We go at God's pace, keep rhythms of prayer, Scripture and sabbath, and deal with things honestly. We pursue Jesus first, ahead of church success.

    In 2030Leaders who are well with God, with others and in themselves.

  5. 05

    We are next-gen focused

    The most important thing isn't what we achieve but who we raise. We partner with parents, give young leaders a go early, and make sure every young person is known and loved by a leader.

    In 2030Kids, youth and young adults each 200 strong, leading worship, preaching and reaching their friends.

  6. 06

    We are multiplying

    We multiply new believers, new leaders and new churches. We help each other invite, we raise leaders for the church and the marketplace, and we plant and revitalise churches together as one family.

    In 2030Ten people a month giving their lives to Jesus, and four locations working as one.

Our discipleship strategy

Think of it as a house.

Every person has a next step. We picture it as moving through a home, from the front door all the way to the kitchen.

  1. 01

    Front Door

    Hello!

    Where people first meet us. Sundays, events, Alpha and a warm welcome.

  2. 02

    Table

    Join us!

    Where people find their place. A small group, a serving team and real friendships.

  3. 03

    Living Room

    Go deeper!

    Where faith grows. Foundations, baptism, freedom and going deep in God's Word.

  4. 04

    Kitchen

    Help us cook!

    Where people lead. Stepping in, stepping up and stepping out to lead others.
