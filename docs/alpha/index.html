A night at Alpha

What happens at Alpha?

Take a break from the rush of the week. Meet new people, consider a fresh perspective and make room for the questions you don't often get to ask.

  1. 01

    Eat together

    Good conversation often starts with good food. We begin with a free dinner and time to get to know each other. Come with a friend or meet one here.

  2. 02

    Explore together

    Who is Jesus? Why pray? What does faith have to do with everyday life? A short film gives us something to think about and talk through.

  3. 03

    Talk together

    This is your space to be curious. Bring a different opinion, share what's on your mind or just take it all in. We'd love to hear your perspective.

Curiosity belongs here

Everyone is welcome.

Maybe faith is new to you. Maybe you've tried church before, or you're wondering what you believe now. You don't need the right words or a particular background to join us.

You don't have to know what you believe to start exploring.

Good to know

Wondering about the details?

Got another question? We'd love to hear it. Email alpha@lifepoint.org.au.

Do I have to be a Christian?

No. You can be sceptical, uncertain, interested or somewhere in between. Alpha gives you room to consider the Christian faith and make up your own mind.

Does it cost anything?

Not a thing. Dinner, the evenings together and child-minding are all free.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. We offer free child-minding so you can settle in and enjoy the evening. Just let us know you'll need it when you get in touch.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. Exploring something new can feel easier with someone you know. Tell us who you're bringing so we can make room at the table.

Will I have to speak?

Only if you want to. You won't be put on the spot. There's room to talk, but there's just as much room to listen.

Where and when does Alpha run?

Our current course runs on Sunday evenings, 6.30 to 8.30pm, at the Lifepoint Church Community Hub in Rothwell through to 6 December 2026. From 2027, Alpha will run at the same time in two places: Rothwell and Lifepoint Caboolture West. Email the team to ask about joining or the next course.

How old do I need to be?

Our Alpha evenings are for anyone aged 16 or over.

The course has started. Can I still come?

Send us a message. We'll help you work out whether to come along now or join us when the next course begins.
