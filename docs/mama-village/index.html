Mama Village

Wednesday mornings, 9.30 to 11.30am, during the school term.

Call Casey →

Come as you are

You don't have to do motherhood alone.

Mama Village is a community-run village of mums from all walks of life who want to connect with and support one another through the hard job that is motherhood.

We meet weekly on Wednesday mornings from 9.30 to 11.30am during term time. The location alternates each fortnight between Casey Garthe's house and a local park.

There are plenty of fun activities to keep the little ones busy, and an open space for mums to chat, drink coffee, do life together and just be.

Whether you're thriving, barely surviving or in a good groove, we'd love you to come along for the ride. Come as you are and be part of our Mama Village.
