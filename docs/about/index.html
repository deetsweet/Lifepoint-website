Our mission

Thousands of lives transformed by the good news of Jesus.

Our heart is to make Jesus known in Moreton Bay and see thousands of lives transformed by the power of the gospel.

We believe that as people encounter Jesus, everything changes, and we're committed to being a church that shares his hope with boldness, practical love and authenticity.

Who we are

We are for Moreton Bay.

We love our city and every person who lives and works here. We believe God has placed us here to help our city thrive and serve the greater good of our community.

We don't just talk about doing good, we do it. Every year we give thousands to local organisations and families who need support, and our people serve in countless ways across our region.

Who we are

We are a welcoming community.

We are a community where you can come as you are and find your people.

Whether you're exploring faith or have been following Jesus for years, you'll find a place to belong, grow and be part of something bigger than yourself. Everyone is welcome, and everyone matters.

Who we are

We believe God can transform your life.

We believe the message of Jesus changes everything. No matter who you are or what your story looks like, God offers forgiveness, hope and a new beginning through Jesus.

We've seen lives healed, families restored, and people discover freedom they never thought possible. The same God who's changed our lives can change yours, and we'd love to walk that journey with you.

Who is Jesus?

The centre of everything.

Jesus is the Son of God and the Saviour of the world. He lived a perfect life, died on the cross for our sins, and rose again to offer forgiveness, hope and eternal life to all who believe in him.

Our values

What we're like.

Our church culture is shaped by three value pillars.

An Inviting Family

A place you feel accepted and can't wait to invite others into

Open. Anyone can belong and feel safe and welcomed. We expect new people to be there, and we're prepared for them.

Vibrant. There's a tangible sense of relaxed, joyful fun and life in our family.

Authentic. We're down to earth, honest and real, and we don't change depending on who we're around.

A House of Prayer

A place where God is central, praised and needed

Dependent. We prioritise our relationship with God above all else. We come to him to receive all we need, and rely on the Holy Spirit in all we do.

Confident. We pray with bold, simple faith, knowing our God is listening and is for us. We trust God even when his plans don't make sense yet.

Worshippers. We're overwhelmed by the greatness and love of God, and live in humble worship in response to his grace.

A Light to the World

People who live to love others and point them to Jesus

Generous. We give joyfully and abundantly of our time and resources, holding our finances and possessions lightly in response to God's goodness.

Inviters. We invite people to discover Jesus.

Equippers. We equip people to be a light in their own circumstances.

Our story

The Lifepoint journey.

Our church was planted in 2007 when a small group of young adults and families from a city campus started meeting in a cinema at Strathpine. Their heart was to start a church where regular Aussies could hear the gospel, and our passion is the same today.

Lifepoint is now a growing, multigenerational church where anyone is welcome to explore faith in a safe and welcoming space.

We believe church isn't a club for good religious people to feel better about themselves. It's a family of real and imperfect people trying to find what it means to be truly human.

We believe the answer to our deepest desires, questions and problems is found in the life and message of Jesus. No matter your stage of life or your beliefs, there's a space for you in our community. We have teenagers, young adults, families, singles and seniors, and we'd love to meet you too.

Explore this website, especially our ministries for every age and stage, or watch a message online to get a feel for what we're like. Or even better, visit us this Sunday!

More about us

What you'll find here.

A church that's very much alive

Lifepoint is a growing and vibrant church in Moreton Bay. Whoever said the church is dying forgot to tell these people. We're known in this region for being incredibly welcoming and strangely authentic. People just seem real, even if they're messy.

It's all about Jesus

We always talk about Jesus, and we believe he is the way, the truth and the life. We believe he was who he claimed to be, and that through him everyone is being called into God's family. God is calling you to be forgiven, redeemed and sent.

Something real is happening here

There's a tangible and undeniable sense of God being alive at Lifepoint. We love to pray and sing, experience God's presence and love, and serve one another.

Bible people in a modern world

We believe the Bible is true and helpful. We love the Bible, preach the Bible, read the Bible, and most importantly, live by its often counter-cultural teaching.

More than Sundays

We don't talk much about rules and religion. We talk about love, grace, truth, and a free and joyful way to live through the power of the Holy Spirit.

A church of groups

Lifepoint is a church of groups. We have dozens of small groups, and we take the home as seriously as our larger gatherings.

Friendship at the heart

In a world where people have fewer people who really know them, Lifepoint is full of friendship. People genuinely love each other, even though they don't agree about everything and come from different social, cultural and religious backgrounds.

A church for the next generation

There are so many young people here! We have hundreds of kids, teenagers and young adults who love church and are being trained to make a difference in this world for good.

A church of generosity and service

Lifepoint people get together all the time to serve people in need, people with disability, our schools and those who are struggling, with real help in financial and other ways. People call on us because they know their mess will be met with grace and love, not judgement.

A church that equips and sends leaders

We're a training church, focused on loving God and loving others where people already live and work. There are a lot of leaders here.

Our family of churches

Lifepoint is a Queensland Baptist church.

Lifepoint is a proud member of the QB movement. Queensland Baptist churches are known for their love for Jesus, taking the Bible seriously, and being people of genuine faith and love in their communities.

Each Sunday, more than 35,000 men, women and children meet in Queensland Baptist churches across our state to worship God. What a celebration!
