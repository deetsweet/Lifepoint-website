A sabbatical is a strange gift. For a season, many of the meetings, decisions and responsibilities that normally fill your week go quiet. At first, that quiet can feel uncomfortable. But over time, God uses it to show you what has been happening beneath the surface.

## 1. Rest is an act of trust

Stopping exposed how easily I can believe that everything depends on me. Rest is not laziness. It is a way of remembering that God is God, I am not, and his work continues even when I step away.

## 2. Presence matters more than productivity

Some of the most valuable moments were wonderfully unproductive: an unhurried conversation, a slow meal, a walk with no agenda, time to listen to God. People rarely need the most efficient version of us. They need us to be truly present.

## 3. Come back with open hands

Time away did not give me a perfect plan for the future. It gave me a renewed willingness to listen. I want to return grateful for what has been, attentive to what God is doing now, and ready to release whatever he asks us to leave behind. The next season belongs to him.

**Jason**
