Questions about giving

Let's keep it simple.

Do I need to give money to the church?

No. Giving is voluntary and never a condition of belonging. Christians give in response to God's love and generosity, not out of pressure or obligation.

How much should I give?

Pray, consider your circumstances and choose an amount you can give willingly and responsibly. A tithe (10%) is a guide some Christians use, rather than a compulsory payment or a limit on generosity. See Malachi 3:10. You can also listen to our message on financial freedom.

Can I give regularly?

Yes. You can set up a recurring bank transfer using the account details above. For online giving, visit our ChurchSuite donation page to see the options.

Can I give to the Building Fund or Lifepoint Caboolture West?

Yes. Both have their own bank account listed above. For the Building Fund, you can also complete a pledge form. Find out more about Lifepoint Caboolture West.

What does the Bible say about generosity?

Generosity flows from gratitude for what God has given us. 2 Corinthians 9:6-11 encourages willing, cheerful giving and trust in God's provision. Giving isn't a transaction or a promise of personal wealth. It's a way to worship God, care for others and share what we have.

How can I ask about church finances?

We welcome questions about giving, our budget and how funds are used. Contact our team and we'll connect you with the right person.

Thank you for your generosity.
