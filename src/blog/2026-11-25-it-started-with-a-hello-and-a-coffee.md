Hey Lifepoint,

I wanted to share a quick, rough transcript of a conversation I had with someone on a Sunday a couple of weeks ago (names left out).

<div class="chat">

**Me:** Hi, I don't think I've met you yet. I'm Jason.

**Them:** Hi Jason. No, I'm new here.

**Me:** That's cool, good to meet you. Is this your first time?

**Them:** No, this is my second time.

**Me:** Nice, well it's great to have you back.

**Them:** To be honest, I wasn't actually going to come back. It was only because of [a Team Lifepoint person] that I did.

**Me:** Oh wow, OK. What did they do?

**Them:** Well, I find it harder in bigger crowds. I was on my own and about to leave, but then they came and introduced themselves, chatted to me and then took me over to get a coffee.

</div>

We chatted a bit more, but I share that with you, church, because that Team Lifepoint person is on our Guest Team. We call it a "guest" team because, just like you'd make a guest feel welcome in your home and show them around, our Guest Team helps people feel welcome on a Sunday.

It's only because of them that this new person came back. I didn't get to find out much about their faith journey, but that Team Lifepoint person might even be the reason that new person one day meets Jesus!

So here's your invitation: why not try out one of our teams at our [Christmas Festival](/events/)? We'd love to have you. I find it an absolute privilege to serve alongside them all, and I'm certain they're storing up for themselves treasure in heaven (Matthew 6:19–21). Come and join us!

[Find a team you're interested in](/teams/) and we'll be in touch.

Thanks Lifepoint,

**Jason**
