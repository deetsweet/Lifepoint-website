Your first Sunday

Here's how it flows.

  1. 01

    Arrive

    Aim for about 10 minutes early. Park free beside the Chapel and look for our welcome team at the door.

  2. 02

    If you have kids

    Our Next Gen team will get your children checked in and settled, then show you where to pick them up.

  3. 03

    Gather

    We sing, pray and hear a message from the Bible. The whole service runs for about 65 minutes.

  4. 04

    Stay for a coffee

    If it's your first time, your barista coffee is on us. It's the easiest way to meet a few people.

A welcome from Dan

"You don't need to know the songs, own a Bible or have it all worked out. Just come. We'd love to meet you."

Walking into a church for the first time can feel like a big step. I get that. Every week we have people with us who are just checking things out, people coming back after a long break, and people who've followed Jesus for decades.

Good to know

The practical stuff.

Free parking

Right beside the Chapel.

Accessible site

Our site is accessible, with disability parking available.

Parents' room

A quiet space if you need it with your little one.

No dress code

Wear whatever you're comfortable in. Most people dress like they're heading to the shops.

Got questions?

Good questions.

Still wondering about something? Email hello@lifepoint.org.au or call 0488 854 336. A real person will get back to you.

Do I have to be a Christian to come?

Not at all. Every Sunday we have people who wouldn't call themselves Christian or religious. You're welcome to come, look around and ask questions.

What happens to my kids?

At the 8.30am and 10am services we run Tots, Kids Crew Jnr and Kids Crew during school terms. In school holidays kids join their families in the service. Check-in is in the foyer, or pre-register online to skip the forms.

How long is a service?

About 65 minutes. We sing, pray together, hear what's happening around the church and listen to a message from the Bible of around 25 minutes.

Will I have to do anything?

Only what you're comfortable with. We'll stand for some songs and prayers and it will always be clear when, but you're free to sit and take it in.

Do I have to give money?

No. Giving is for people who call Lifepoint home. As a guest, please don't feel any expectation.

Do you have communion?

Yes, every four weeks. Communion, also called the Lord's Supper, is when we share bread and juice to remember Jesus' death for us. Anyone who wants to receive God's grace is welcome to take part, and you're equally welcome to sit it out.

Morning or 5pm?

They're very similar, with the same message and music. The main difference is that kids programs only run in the morning.

How do I find the building?

We meet in the Chapel at Grace Lutheran College, Rothwell. Turn off Anzac Ave into Mewes Rd. The Chapel is the large building first on your left, so turn into the first carpark and you'll see it. There are signs on Sundays too.
