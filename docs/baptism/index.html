Baptism

Our baptism Sundays are the first Sunday of every month.

I want to get baptised →Questions →

<a href="https://www.bible.com/bible/59/ACT.22.16.ESV">Acts 22:16</a>

"Why do you wait? Rise and be baptised and wash away your sins, calling on his name."

We believe baptism by immersion is a vital step in following Jesus. The Bible presents baptism as an outward expression of an inward faith in Jesus Christ alone for salvation. It is an act of obedience to the command and example of Jesus, publicly declaring a believer's faith in and identification with their crucified, buried and risen Saviour.

Frequently asked questions

Wondering about baptism?

Who can get baptised?

Anyone who has put their faith in Jesus by repenting of their sin, believing in him and deciding to follow him as their King.

How old do you have to be?

There is no age limit. The only requirement is that a person can explain the gospel and grasp what they are declaring (see page 3 of the guide for questions to check). We have baptised children as young as 12 who have shown this.

Do I have to be baptised and share my story in front of everyone?

Baptism isn't just between you and God. It is also being joined to God's community of believers, the church. For that reason, baptism usually happens together as a church family on a Sunday. We also encourage people to share their story of meeting Jesus, as a declaration of faith and an encouragement to everyone else.

If this is a big obstacle for you, please talk to us. We believe nothing should stand in the way of baptism and we'll work something out.

What should I say in my testimony?

Just answer these questions:
What was life like before you met Jesus?
How has he changed your life?
How has Lifepoint helped along the way?
Why are you getting baptised?

Who can baptise me?

We believe in the priesthood of all believers, so any believer can baptise another believer. In the New Testament this was usually done by a Christian leader or someone who had been important in the person's faith journey, and we tend to follow that example.

Can I be baptised again?

We don't usually baptise someone who was already baptised as an adult, as there's no need. We can make exceptions if you're unsure about your first baptism, such as whether you were a Christian at the time or understood what was happening.

We do baptise believers who were baptised as infants in another denomination, because that was their parents' decision rather than their own. We see this as confirming and honouring the decision their parents made.

How do I go about getting baptised?

Speak to a pastor, or let us know here and we'll organise a chat with you.
