Our beliefs

What we believe

The core of our faith.

The core of what we believe at Lifepoint. Lifepoint is a member of Queensland Baptists (QB).

One eternal God in three persons

We believe that there is one true God who has existed for eternity as three persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.

The authority of the Bible

We believe in the divine inspiration and absolute authority of the Bible, the written Word of God, fully trustworthy for faith and conduct.

Humanity created in God's image

We believe that God created people in his image, male and female, to share in his eternal life, worship him and steward creation.

The problem of sin

We believe that because of humanity's rebellion against God (we call this sin), everyone has fallen short of the glory of God, is driven by their fallen nature and is eternally separated from him.

The incarnation of Jesus Christ

We believe that God showed his great love for us by sending his Son, Jesus Christ, in human form through the incarnation, born of the virgin Mary. Jesus was without sin, fully God and fully man.

Christ's atoning sacrifice

We believe in the atoning sacrifice of Christ on the cross, paying the price of sin (separation from God), defeating evil and death, and reconciling us to God.

The resurrection and ascension

We believe that Jesus physically rose from the dead on the third day, and that he ascended to the Father's right hand, where he now reigns as the Saviour of the world.

Justification by faith

We believe that fallen humans are justified through faith in this Saviour, Jesus Christ.

The work of the Holy Spirit

We believe that God sent his comforter and guide, the Holy Spirit, who leads us into repentance and life with Christ through new birth, empowers our Christian life, deepens our faith and enables us to witness to the world.

The Church and its mission

We believe that God has established his people as the Church, his priesthood of all believers, empowered by the Holy Spirit through spiritual gifts, and called to proclaim the gospel, promote justice and live out his Kingdom life on earth.

The return of Christ and final judgement

We believe that Christ will personally and visibly return to fulfil God's salvation plan. He will raise all people to judgement, in which the righteous will enter eternal life with him and the rebellious eternal separation, and he will finally establish a new heaven and a new earth.
