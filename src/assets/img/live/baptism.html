BAPTISM

A PUBLIC DECLARATION OF FAITH IN JESUS CHRIST

I WANT TO GET BAPTISED

"Why do you wait? Rise and be baptised and wash away your sins, calling on his name."

- Acts 22:16


We believe baptism by immersion is a vital step in following Jesus. The Bible presents baptism as an outward expression of an inward faith in Jesus Christ alone for salvation. Baptism is an act of obedience to the command and example of Jesus: declaring publicly the believer’s faith in and identification with their crucified, buried, and risen Saviour.



Our Baptism Course

We have a short and simple course to help you understand what you are getting yourself into, what the Bible says about baptism, and also to help you prepare for your baptism day.

Frequently Asked Questions about Baptism

Who can get baptised?

Anyone who has put their faith in Jesus by repenting of their sin, believing in him, and has decided to follow him as their King.

How old do you have to be to be baptised?

There is no age limit on baptism. The only requirement is that a person can articulate the Gospel and grasp what they are declaring (see page 3 for questions to check). We have baptised children as young as 12 who have demonstrated this.

Do I have to get baptised and share my testimony in front of everyone?

Baptism is not just ‘between you and God’, it is immersion into God’s community of believers called the Church. For this reason, baptism is usually done together as a church family on a Sunday. We also encourage those wanting to be baptised to share their story of meeting Jesus as a declaration of their faith and as an encouragement to the rest of the family. If this is a huge problem stopping you from getting baptised, we believe nothing should stand in the way of baptism, so please speak to us and we will work something out!

What should I say in my testimony?

Just answer these three questions!

What was life like before you met Jesus?

How has he changed your life?

How has Lifepoint helped along the journey?

Why are you getting baptised?

Who can baptise me?

We believe in the priesthood of all believers, so any believer can baptise another believer. In the New Testament this is usually done by a Christian leader or someone who has been instrumental in your faith journey, so we tend to follow this example too.

Can I be baptised again?

We do not usually baptise someone who has already been baptised as an adult as there is no need. But we can make exceptions if someone is unsure of their first baptism, and whether they were a Christian at the time or understood what was happening.

We do baptise believers who were baptised as an infant in another denomination as that was a decision made by their parents, not them. We see this as confirming and honouring the decision made by their parents.

How do I go about getting baptised?

Speak to a Pastor, or contact us below and we will organise to have a chat to with you.

More about us...

Lifepoint is a growing and vibrant church in Moreton Bay. Whoever said that the church is dying forgot to tell these people. It is a place and people known in this region for being incredibly welcoming and strangely authentic, people just seem real (even if they are messy).

We always talk about Jesus, and we believe he is the way, the truth and the life. We believe he was who he claimed to be, and that through him everyone is being called into God's family. We believe that God is calling you to be forgiven, redeemed and sent!

There is a tangible and undeniable sense of God being alive at Lifepoint. We love to pray and sing, experience God's presence and love and serve one another. 

We believe that the Bible is true and helpful. We love the Bible, preach the Bible, read the Bible, and most importantly, live by its often counter-cultural teaching.

We don’t talk much about rules and religion at Lifepoint, we talk about love, grace, truth, and a strange new free and joyful way to live that involves a supernatural power called the Holy Spirit.

Lifepoint is a church of groups. We have dozens of small groups and we take the home as seriously as we do our larger gatherings.

In a world where people have fewer people who really know them, Lifepoint is full of friendship. It is refreshing to see people who genuinely love each other, even though they all don’t agree about everything and come from different social, cultural and religious backgrounds.

There are so many young people here! We have hundreds of kids, teenagers and young adults at Lifepoint. Who would have thought it but these young people LOVE church, and are being trained as people who will make a difference in this world for good.

Lifepoint people get together in groups all the time and serve our needy, our disabled, our schools, the struggling, and offer real help in financial and other ways. It is a place people call upon because they know their mess will be met not with judgement, but grace and love.

It is a training church, with the main focus being on loving God and loving others where people are already working and living. There are a very high number of leaders here.


Lifepoint is a Queensland Baptist Church

Lifepoint is a proud member of the QB Movement. Queensland Baptist churches are known for their love for Jesus, taking the Bible seriously, and for being people of genuine faith and love in their community.  

Each Sunday, more than 35,000 men, women and children meet in Queensland Baptist churches throughout our state to praise and worship God. What a celebration!
