A message from our planting pastor

Hi, I'm Breyton.

I'm leading Lifepoint's new campus in Waraba (Caboolture West). My wife Georgie and I live in the area, and we're passionate about this community and seeing lives transformed by the good news of Jesus.

Waraba is growing fast, with thousands of families moving into streets that were paddocks not long ago. Most of them arrive not knowing anyone, and we want to be the first to say hello. We want every one of those families to find friendship, hope and a real relationship with Jesus.

We're building a launch team ahead of our public launch on 7 February 2027. If you live nearby, we'd love to meet you. Get in touch and I'll be in contact soon.

Breyton MannPlanting Pastor, Lifepoint Caboolture West

Waraba in numbers

A whole new community.

We're getting ready to launch a church in the Waraba (Caboolture West) region. West of Caboolture, the Waraba Priority Development Area is planned to grow over around 40 years, with new homes, local jobs, schools, parks and community facilities.

More about us

Get to know Lifepoint.

A church that's very much alive

Lifepoint is a growing and vibrant church in Moreton Bay. We're known in this region for being incredibly welcoming and strangely authentic. People just seem real, even if they're messy.

It's all about Jesus

We always talk about Jesus, and we believe he is the way, the truth and the life. God is calling you to be forgiven, redeemed and sent.

Something real is happening here

There's a tangible sense of God being alive at Lifepoint. We love to pray and sing, experience God's presence and love, and serve one another.

A church of groups

We have dozens of small groups, and we take the home as seriously as our larger gatherings.

A church for the next generation

We have hundreds of kids, teenagers and young adults who love church and are being trained to make a difference in this world for good.
