## What is fasting, and why should we do it?

Fasting (going without food for a time) is an ancient practice of turning and drawing closer to God, reawakening our hunger for him, and seeking him for spiritual breakthrough or guidance. The Bible gives many examples of God's people combining fasting with prayer to stir up their passion for God, seek his help in a special way, and renew their devotion and closeness to their Creator. King David wrote that he "humbled himself with fasting" (Psalm 35:13).

The Bible records that great heroes of faith such as Moses, Daniel, Paul and Jesus himself fasted so that they might draw close to God (Exodus 34:28, Daniel 9:3, 2 Corinthians 11:27, Matthew 4:2). Jesus knew that his followers, once he was no longer with them in the flesh, would at times need to fast to regain and renew their passion to serve him (Mark 2:18–20).

Fasting always goes together with prayer. If you're not praying while fasting, you're only dieting!

## Frequently asked questions

### Is fasting from food dangerous for my health?

Within reason, fasting is safe, and some say that for limited times it's even healthy. But for some people it might be harmful, for example during illness or pregnancy, for anyone with an eating disorder, or for children. If you're unsure, check with a doctor. If you haven't fasted before, start small by skipping one meal or trying a 24-hour fast.

### Can I fast from something other than food?

Fasting in the Bible usually means going without food, so that should probably be the first option if you can. For some people that's medically unwise or impossible, though, and giving up other enjoyable things like social media or TV can work well too.

### Do Christians have to fast?

There's no clear command in the New Testament that you have to fast, so technically, no. But it does seem to be assumed that God's people will fast as a normal part of following Jesus ("When you fast…", Matthew 6:16). It's a clear path to seeking more of our incredible God, and who doesn't want that? It has also been a regular practice for devoted Christians for centuries.

## Practical tips

- **Have a purpose.** Decide clearly why you're fasting, and keep coming back to that purpose.
- **Start small.** Begin by skipping one meal a day for a few days in a row.
- **Pray instead of eating.** Use the time to pray and be filled by God instead of something else.
- **Pray when hunger comes.** Let hunger pangs turn your mind and attention to God.
- **Keep a journal.** It can help to write down what God is showing you.
- **Don't draw attention to your fast.** You may need to tell some people for practical reasons, but don't talk about it all the time or make it a big deal.
