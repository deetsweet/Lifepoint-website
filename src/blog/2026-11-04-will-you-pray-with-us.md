Hey Lifepoint! It's Breyton Mann here, the future campus pastor of Lifepoint Caboolture West.

As a church, we're planting a new Lifepoint campus in Caboolture West. It will be the same church, with the same vision and heart, meeting in a second location. Lifepoint Caboolture West launches publicly on **Sunday 7 February 2027** at Grace College Caboolture, and we're sending a team from here to make it happen. If you're interested in joining the team, you can [find out more about Lifepoint Caboolture West](/waraba/).

God has been doing so much in the lead-up. We've seen generous donations, gifted sound gear and confirmation of our funding. We have a leadership team of ten young, passionate Christians, and Josh Cabading has now come on as our Kids Coordinator. More than forty Lifepointers, on the way to sixty, are giving a year to help the plant. We're calling them the "Super Sixty". Many volunteers have also stepped up into new leadership roles. Multiplication is well underway, and the King of Kings is leading all of it.

But today, more than anything, I want to ask you to join us in prayer. The core team, our key leaders, the Super Sixty and I are working hard to get Lifepoint Caboolture West ready to reach the Caboolture community. This is a prayer that came to me in our last prayer meeting. Would you spend some time today praying with us? Thank you.

<div class="prayer">

Lord, we are honoured to carry your name.

Steer our hearts towards Caboolture and those there who need you.

Lord, continue to build your church through us, for our own labour is in vain without you. Guide us to get this community ready for them.

Let them come as they are, rest their weary bones, and be refreshed by their Creator, with us.

We pray to see thousands in our region have their lives transformed by you, O God.

Amen.

</div>

**Breyton**
