Young Adults

A community for anyone from fresh out of school to settling into their 30s, exploring Jesus and doing life together.

A place to grow

Find your people.

Young adulthood can be exciting, and sometimes challenging. You don't have to work it out on your own.

Whether you're studying, working or figuring out what comes next, we're here to help you explore Jesus in community. Here are three ways to get involved.

01 · Sundays

Make Sunday part of your week.

Most of our young adults gather at the 5pm Sunday service. We worship, hear a message from the Bible and spend time together.

Come from 4.30pm for Qadash. A space to slow down, pray and worship together before the service begins.

Grace Lutheran College Chapel, Rothwell
Mewes Road, off Anzac Avenue

Plan your Sunday →

02 · Wednesdays

Wednesday groups.

Wednesday nights during the school term are about growing in faith and building friendships in a smaller group.

Wednesday groups

Under 20s

Meet weekly during term at the Lifepoint Church Community Hub, 1 McKillop Street, Rothwell.

New to Young Adults? Get in touch with Zac and we'll help you connect.

Get directions → Ask Zac →

Wednesday groups

Over 20s

Connect with one of our small groups meeting across Moreton Bay.

There's space to build friendships, explore the Bible and encourage each other through the everyday stuff of life.

Find a group → Ask Zac →

03 · Beyond the weekly rhythm

Retreats, socials and more.

Across the year we make space to get away, have fun, worship and serve together.

  • Encounter prayer and worship nights
  • Yearly retreat in August
  • Socials
  • Group service opportunities
