Baptism

Our baptism Sundays are the first Sunday of every month.

I want to get baptised →Questions →

Acts 22:16

"Why do you wait? Rise and be baptised and wash away your sins, calling on his name."

We believe baptism by immersion is a vital step in following Jesus. The Bible presents baptism as an outward expression of an inward faith in Jesus Christ alone for salvation. Baptism is an act of obedience to the command and example of Jesus, declaring publicly the believer's faith in and identification with their crucified, buried and risen Saviour.

Our baptism course.

We have a short and simple course to help you understand what you are getting yourself into, what the Bible says about baptism, and to help you prepare for your baptism day.

There are four videos with an accompanying PDF for you to go through with a trusted friend or mentor from Lifepoint Church.

Frequently asked questions

Wondering about baptism?

Who can get baptised?

Anyone who has put their faith in Jesus by repenting of their sin, believing in him and deciding to follow him as their King.

How old do you have to be?

There is no age limit. The only requirement is that a person can explain the gospel and grasp what they are declaring (see page 3 of the guide for questions to check). We have baptised children as young as 12 who have shown this.

Do I have to be baptised and share my story in front of everyone?

Baptism isn't just between you and God. It is also being joined to God's community of believers, the church. For that reason, baptism usually happens together as a church family on a Sunday. We also encourage people to share their story of meeting Jesus, as a declaration of faith and an encouragement to everyone else.

If this is a big obstacle for you, please talk to us. We believe nothing should stand in the way of baptism and we'll work something out.

What should I say in my testimony?

Just answer these questions:
What was life like before you met Jesus?
How has he changed your life?
How has Lifepoint helped along the way?
Why are you getting baptised?

Who can baptise me?

We believe in the priesthood of all believers, so any believer can baptise another believer. In the New Testament this was usually done by a Christian leader or someone who had been important in the person's faith journey, and we tend to follow that example.

Can I be baptised again?

We don't usually baptise someone who was already baptised as an adult, as there's no need. We can make exceptions if you're unsure about your first baptism, such as whether you were a Christian at the time or understood what was happening.

We do baptise believers who were baptised as infants in another denomination, because that was their parents' decision rather than their own. We see this as confirming and honouring the decision their parents made.

How do I go about getting baptised?

Speak to a pastor, or let us know here and we'll organise a chat with you.

More about us.

A church that's very much alive

Lifepoint is a growing and vibrant church in Moreton Bay. Whoever said that the church is dying forgot to tell these people. It is a place and people known in this region for being incredibly welcoming and strangely authentic. People just seem real, even if they are messy.

It's all about Jesus

We always talk about Jesus, and we believe he is the way, the truth and the life. We believe he was who he claimed to be, and that through him everyone is being called into God's family. We believe that God is calling you to be forgiven, redeemed and sent!

Something real is happening here

There is a tangible and undeniable sense of God being alive at Lifepoint. We love to pray and sing, experience God's presence and love and serve one another.

Bible people in a modern world

We believe that the Bible is true and helpful. We love the Bible, preach the Bible, read the Bible and, most importantly, live by its often counter-cultural teaching.

More than Sundays

We don't talk much about rules and religion at Lifepoint. We talk about love, grace, truth, and a strange new free and joyful way to live that involves a supernatural power called the Holy Spirit.

A church of groups

Lifepoint is a church of groups. We have dozens of small groups and we take the home as seriously as we do our larger gatherings.

Friendship at the heart

In a world where people have fewer people who really know them, Lifepoint is full of friendship. It is refreshing to see people who genuinely love each other, even though they don't all agree about everything and come from different social, cultural and religious backgrounds.

A church for the next generation

There are so many young people here! We have hundreds of kids, teenagers and young adults at Lifepoint. Who would have thought it, but these young people love church and are being trained as people who will make a difference in this world for good.

A church of generosity and service

Lifepoint people get together in groups all the time and serve our needy, our disabled, our schools and the struggling, and offer real help in financial and other ways. It is a place people call on because they know their mess will be met not with judgement, but with grace and love.

A church that equips and sends leaders

It is a training church, with the main focus on loving God and loving others where people are already working and living. There are a very high number of leaders here.

Lifepoint is a Queensland Baptist church.

Lifepoint is a proud member of the QB movement. Queensland Baptist churches are known for their love for Jesus, taking the Bible seriously, and being people of genuine faith and love in their community.

Each Sunday more than 35,000 men, women and children meet in Queensland Baptist churches throughout our state to praise and worship God. What a celebration!
