Faith. Friends. Real life.

YoungAdults

Open, vibrant, authentic. A community to explore Jesus and share life, from fresh out of school to settling into your 30s.

Get connectedWednesday groups
Sundays at 5pmWednesday groupsEvents & shared moments

A place to grow

Find your people.
Explore your faith.

Young adulthood can be exciting, and sometimes challenging. You don’t have to navigate it on your own.

Whether you’re studying, working or figuring out what comes next, we’re here to help you explore Jesus in community. Here are three ways to get involved.

01 · Sundays

Make Sunday
part of your week.

Our young adults mostly gather at the 5pm Sunday service. We worship, hear a message from the Bible and spend time together.

Come from 4.30pm for Qadash.
A space to slow down, pray and worship together before the service begins.

Grace College Chapel, Rothwell
Mewes Road, off Anzac Avenue
Plan your Sunday

02 · Wednesdays

Go a little deeper.
Do life together.

Wednesday nights during the school term are about growing in faith and building friendships in a smaller group.

Wednesday Groups

Under 20s

Meet weekly during term time at the Lifepoint Hub.

1 McKillop Street, Rothwell
New to Young Adults? Get in touch with Zac and we’ll help you connect.

Get directionsAsk Zac

Wednesday Groups

Over 20s

Connect with one of our small groups meeting across Moreton Bay.

There’s space to build friendships, explore the Bible and encourage each other through the everyday stuff of life.

Find a groupAsk Zac

03 · Beyond the weekly rhythm

Shared moments.
Lasting friendships.

Across the year, we make space to get away, have fun, worship and serve together.

  • Encounter prayer & worship nights
  • Yearly retreat in August
  • Socials
  • Group service opportunities
