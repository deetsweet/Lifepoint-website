Care at Lifepoint

What do you need?

Connection?

We all need friends. One of the best ways to find genuine connection at Lifepoint is through small groups. Whether you’re looking for friendship, meaningful community, or just a place to belong, there’s a group for you!

Join a Group

Prayer?

No matter what you're going through, we deeply believe in the power of prayer. Our prayer team would love to stand with you, encourage your heart, speak hope over your life, and commit your needs to God.

Submit a Prayer Request

Questions about faith?

If you have questions about faith, God, or the meaning of life, Alpha is a great place to start. It's a relaxed, open space where you can explore big questions and discover who Jesus is.

Find Out About Alpha

Need counselling?

Sometimes, we need guidance and encouragement to navigate life's challenges. We’ve gathered sermon resources for self-guided support, and we can also recommend some local professional counselors if you need deeper care.

Find A Counsellor

Life Coaching?

Sometimes life can make you feel like you are in a long term tough spot. Thanks to our amazing partners Carinity and COACH Network Australia, we have trained coaches who can help you clarify your goals and take the next steps forward. If you're interested in working with a coach, contact us using the link below to let us know!

Contact Us

Financial Mentoring?

Struggling with budgeting, debt, or financial stress? We offer financial mentoring through CAP (Christians Against Poverty) to help you get on top of managing your finances. If you're interested in finding out more, contact us using the link below to let us know!

Contact Us

Food, Accommodation & Financial Help

We encourage you to seek out support from these trusted organisations: 

Ask Izzy: https://askizzy.org.au/ 

Food assistance locally 

Kingdom makers: http://kingdommakers.com/food-outlet

Brekkie club: https://thebreakfastclubredcliffe.org.au/

Encircle financial assistance, counselling, housing support: https://encircle.org.au/encircle-redcliffe-neighbourhood-centre/

Need More Support?

If the resources and opportunities above don’t quite meet your care needs, please let us know how we might be able to help. We’ll do our best to support you, and if we can’t, we’ll try to connect you with someone who can.

Connect With Us

Keen to help?

Alternatively, if you'd like to join the care team and help us care for our community, use the link below to let us know.

Join the Care Team
