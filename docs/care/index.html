Care at Lifepoint

Whatever you're facing, you don't have to face it alone.

What do you need? →Contact us →

We're here to help

What do you need?

Connection

We all need friends. One of the best ways to find real connection at Lifepoint is through a small group. Whether you're looking for friendship, community or a place to belong, there's a group for you.

Join a group →

Prayer

Whatever you're going through, we believe in the power of prayer. Our prayer team would love to stand with you, encourage you, speak hope over your life and bring your needs to God.

Ask for prayer →

Questions about faith

If you have questions about faith, God or the meaning of life, Alpha is a great place to start. It's a relaxed, open space to explore big questions and discover who Jesus is.

Find out about Alpha →

Counselling

Sometimes we need guidance and encouragement to get through life's challenges. We've gathered sermon resources for self-guided support, and we can recommend local professional counsellors if you need deeper care.

Find a counsellor →

Life coaching

When life feels like a long, tough season, a coach can help. Thanks to our partners Carinity and COACH Network Australia, we have trained coaches who can help you clarify your goals and take your next steps.

Contact us →

Financial mentoring

Struggling with budgeting, debt or financial stress? We offer financial mentoring through CAP (Christians Against Poverty) to help you get on top of your finances.

Contact us →

Local support

Food, accommodation and financial help.

We encourage you to reach out to these trusted organisations:

  • Ask Izzy: find local help of all kinds
  • Kingdom Makers: local food assistance
  • The Breakfast Club Redcliffe: free breakfasts
  • Encircle: financial assistance, counselling and housing support

Need more support?

If the options above don't quite meet your needs, let us know how we can help. We'll do our best to support you, and if we can't, we'll try to connect you with someone who can.

Connect with us →

Keen to help?

If you'd like to join the Care Team and help us care for our community, let us know.

Join the Care Team →
