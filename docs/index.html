A message from our Senior Pastor

You're welcome in our family

Our vision at Lifepoint is to make Jesus known in our region and see thousands of lives transformed by the gospel.

We believe that every person matters to God, and we invite you to our church no matter what your beliefs, doubts, past or problems.

In a world where being completely known and being completely accepted are often two separate categories, we believe the strange and shockingly good message of Jesus is exactly what this world needs right now.

We'd love to see you Sunday.

Dan and Steph Sweetman
