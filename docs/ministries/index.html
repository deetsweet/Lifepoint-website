Programs and community

Kids and young families

We have hundreds of kids at Lifepoint, and we're passionate about creating fun and safe spaces where children can encounter God's grace and learn to follow Jesus.

Youth

Join us on Friday nights during school term for an epic evening of hanging out with friends, sports, games and exploring who Jesus is and what his message means for young people.

Find out more →

Young adults

A thriving group of single and married young adults who meet in small groups during the week and hold events throughout the year.

Find out more →

Seniors

Our seniors are a dynamic and energetic group of young-at-heart retirees who love getting together to grow in friendship and faith.

Small groups

We believe faith and growth happen better in circles than in rows. We're a church of groups, and there's a group waiting for you.

Lifepoint Women

Lifepoint Men

Theology Group

We take the Bible seriously. Each term we host a Bible study series run by biblical scholars on a book we've been studying together.
